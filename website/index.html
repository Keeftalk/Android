About Keeftalk

About Keeftalk: Keeftalk is a secure communication platform that helps users communicate and manage their digital information privately. We utilize Google Sign-In to securely authenticate users and establish their accounts. Users can exchange messages, make voice and video calls, share media files, and organize personal content including notes and schedules from one application.
