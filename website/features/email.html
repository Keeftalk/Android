Unified Interface

Manage multiple email accounts without switching applications. Keeftalk provides a consolidated view of your inbox while keeping data handled locally on your device.

  • Combined inbox management

Data Privacy Controls

Keeftalk uses a local processing engine to manage email data, helping to neutralize common tracking technologies used in external emails. We utilize Google Sign-In to securely authenticate users and integrate with external email providers.

  • Neutralization of tracking pixels
  • Secure handling of attachments
  • Local cache management
