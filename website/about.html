About Keeftalk

Keeftalk is a secure communication application built to provide users with tools to communicate and manage their digital information privately.

Our Mission

To provide a secure, unified communication application that enables individuals to exchange messages, make calls, and manage personal data with a focus on privacy and security. We utilize Google Sign-In to securely authenticate users.

Why Keeftalk?

Keeftalk addresses the need for secure digital communication by combining messaging, voice/video calls, and information management into one application that uses modern security standards.

Based in Tunis, Tunisia, Keeftalk LLC serves professionals and individuals who require secure handling of their digital communications and personal information.
