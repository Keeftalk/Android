# Keeftalk v0.9-Alpha Release

Keeftalk is a privacy-first "Super App" designed to unify your communication, productivity, and digital life within a single, end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) ecosystem. Developed by Keeftalk LLC, it provides a secure sanctuary for your personal information, respecting your data sovereignty.

### 🛡️ Core Philosophy
Built on principles of absolute privacy, Keeftalk ensures you retain full control over your digital content. We operate with zero advertising data, zero data sales, and military-grade encryption standards, including AES-256-GCM, Argon2id, and Signal Protocol-based E2EE.

### 🚀 Key Features
- **Unified Communication:** Secure E2EE messaging, WebRTC voice/video calls, and a fully integrated SMS/MMS carrier client.
- **Hardware-Backed Vault:** Protect sensitive media and documents with biometric-locked, hardware-backed SQLCipher encryption.
- **Integrated Productivity Suite:** Encrypted Markdown notes, PDF & sheet viewer, habit tracker with streaks, e-book reader with Reading XP, calendar agenda, and task manager.
- **Privacy Browser & Sky Engine:** Privacy web browsing with a live-simulated environmental sky and weather background engine.
- **Financial & Market Tracker:** Real-time crypto and stock market charts with watchlists.
- **3D Social Connection Engine:** Interactive 3D graph visualization of network connection paths.
- **Security & Privacy Center:** 2FA (TOTP/Email), offline emergency recovery keys, multi-device session management, and regional cloud storage.

### 🛠️ Technical Highlights
- **Modern Android Stack:** Built with Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Kotlin Coroutines/Flow.
- **Hardware Security:** Utilizes SQLCipher and the Android Keystore system.
- **Target SDK:** Android 16 (API Level 37), Minimum SDK: Android 8.0 (API Level 26).
